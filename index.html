Why Private MD

Primary care should leave room to think.

Many adults do not need more healthcare complexity. They need a physician who has enough time to understand the full picture, explain the tradeoffs, coordinate the moving parts, and stay involved over time.

Private MD was built around that relationship: a deliberately small patient panel, direct physician access, comprehensive adult Internal Medicine, and a model designed for continuity rather than volume.

What exactly is Private MD?

One medical relationship. Three layers of value.

Instead of asking you to decode the difference between Internal Medicine, Direct Primary Care, concierge care, and longevity medicine, Private MD starts with one clear foundation: your ongoing relationship with one internist.

Your physician

Medicine built around a stronger doctor-patient relationship.

Nishant Sahni, MD, MS

Board-Certified Internal Medicine | MS, Immunology | Former Mayo Clinic and University of Minnesota faculty

Dr. Sahni created Private MD around a simple premise: an internist can do better work when there is enough time to know the patient, think carefully, and connect the details across tests, medications, specialists, and life outside the exam room.

His practice remains deliberately small and clinically grounded. He continues to practice hospital medicine, bringing acute and complex-care experience into an ongoing outpatient relationship centered on continuity, prevention, and sound medical judgment.

Longevity, in context

Plan for tomorrow's health without losing sight of today's medicine.

At Private MD, longevity is best understood as an extension of preventive Internal Medicine - not a separate promise to reverse aging or guarantee outcomes.

For appropriate patients, higher tiers may include more detailed cardiometabolic risk assessment, selected genomic information, continuous glucose monitoring, and emerging screening tools. Those decisions should be individualized and interpreted alongside established guideline-based screening.
